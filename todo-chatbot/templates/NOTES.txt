Your Todo Chatbot application has been deployed!

To access your application:

Frontend: http://{{ .Values.frontend.env.BETTER_AUTH_URL | replace "http://" "" | replace "https://" "" }}
Backend API: http://{{ .Values.frontend.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_API_URL | replace "http://" "" | replace "https://" "" }}

To check the status of your pods:
  kubectl get pods --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}

To view application logs:
  Frontend: kubectl logs -l app.kubernetes.io/component=frontend --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}
  Backend: kubectl logs -l app.kubernetes.io/component=backend --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}
  Database: kubectl logs -l app.kubernetes.io/component=postgres --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}
